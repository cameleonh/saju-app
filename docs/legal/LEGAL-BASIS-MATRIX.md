# Legal-Basis Engineering Matrix

This matrix records the intended product basis for counsel review. It is not a legal opinion.

| Processing | Working basis | Engineering rule | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local calculation/storage | User-requested local functionality | No central submission persistence | Enabled |
| Account authentication | Account-service performance and security | Verified email at Cognito; app stores only provider subject and email hash | Counsel review before launch |
| Adult self cloud save | Necessary to provide requested account save/delete/sync | Versioned service disclosure; no forced consent checkbox presented as optional | Counsel review before launch |
| Security logging | Legitimate service security and legal safeguards | No birth input or tokens in logs; 90-day cap | Incident-owner approval |
| Couple/third-party central storage | No approved basis | Rejected by cloud policy | Disabled |
| Under-19 central storage | Product does not offer it | Rejected by cloud policy | Disabled |
| Training, advertising, marketing | No approved purpose or basis | UI absent and accepted optional receipts rejected | Disabled |
| Overseas processing/support | Requires a final Article 28-8 analysis and notice | Cloud save feature flag remains off | Counsel and vendor-register approval |

The public notice must distinguish mandatory service processing from any future consent-based optional processing, in line with PIPA Articles 15 and 22.
