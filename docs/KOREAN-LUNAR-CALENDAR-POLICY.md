# Korean Lunar Calendar Conversion Policy

Status: implemented and regression-locked on 2026-09-24

Policy: `KR-LUNAR-CONVERSION-1.0@1.0.0`

## Scope

This policy converts Korean lunar dates (음력) to Gregorian dates and describes Gregorian dates as Korean lunar dates. It is separate from the Saju pillar policy: conversion produces a normalized solar civil date, while the natal engine independently applies its `Asia/Seoul` civil-time and solar-term rules.

## Data and lineage

- Runtime source: `korean-lunar-calendar@0.4.0`, MIT-licensed.
- The library's project documentation states that its bundled conversion data follows the Korean Astronomy and Space Science Institute standard and links to <https://astro.kasi.re.kr/>. The package documentation uses the acronym “KARI”; the institute's established acronym is KASI. The project is maintained at <https://github.com/usingsky/korean_lunar_calendar_js>.
- The server calls the pinned library directly. The browser uses `chart/lunar-calendar-data.mjs`, generated by `scripts/generate-lunar-calendar-data.mjs` from the same pinned library and checked into the application for offline use.
- The KASI public page was not available during this repair session; the exact source version and package lineage are therefore recorded rather than claiming a direct live KASI API dependency.

## Supported range and behavior

| Calendar | Supported range |
|---|---|
| Lunar input | `1900-01-01` through `2050-11-18` |
| Solar-to-lunar table | `1900-01-31` through `2050-12-31` |

Invalid month/day combinations, impossible leap-month flags, and dates outside these ranges are rejected. Invalid lunar dates are never rolled into a neighboring month. The input time is retained on the converted solar date; unknown time is represented by the existing `12:00` normalization sentinel and remains explicitly marked unknown.

## Regression fixtures

- Korean lunar `2024-01-01` converts to solar `2024-02-10`.
- Korean lunar leap fifth-month `2017-05-01` converts to solar `2017-06-24`; the same civil date is a Chinese lunar sixth-month date, making this a deliberate Korea/China divergence fixture.
- Korean lunar leap fourth-month `2020-04-01` converts to solar `2020-05-23`.
- The supported lunar endpoint `2050-11-18` converts to solar `2050-12-31`.
- `tests/unit/solar-lunar.mjs` checks server/table parity across more than 10,000 solar dates and pins the divergence and leap-month boundaries.

This policy is intentionally limited to the named data lineage and range. It does not extrapolate beyond the bundled calendar data or treat Chinese lunar output as interchangeable with Korean lunar output.
